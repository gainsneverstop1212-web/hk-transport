私隱政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 25 日

  • 唔登入都用得，所有資料只存喺你部機。
  • 你嘅即時位置只喺部機計，用嚟搵附近車站同計出門時間，唔會上傳。
  • 登入咗（Google），你嘅設定、屋企／公司位置、常用路線同通勤紀錄會同步到你自己嘅雲端帳戶，只有你睇到。
  • 冇廣告、冇追蹤、冇分析工具，唔會賣或者分享你嘅資料。
  • 隨時可以喺 app 入面刪除帳戶，雲端資料即刻刪除。

1. 呢份政策講乜

「港住走 Rushing HK」（下稱「app」）係一個香港公共交通到站同出門倒數工具，有網頁版同 App Store 版。呢份政策講明 app 收集乜嘢資料、點用、存喺邊同點刪除。負責營運 app 嘅係 【待填：營運者名稱】（下稱「我哋」）。

2. 唔登入：資料只存喺你部機

你喺 app 入面設定嘅嘢，全部存喺部機嘅瀏覽器儲存空間（App 版就係 app 自己嘅儲存空間），我哋睇唔到。包括：

  • 語言、字體大小、步速等設定
  • 常用地點：屋企、學校／公司同自訂地點嘅名稱、位置（經緯度）、樓層、有冇𨋢
  • 常用路線、最近搜尋過嘅路線同目的地
  • 通勤紀錄：你用出門倒數撳「出咗門」嘅時間、起點、目的地同搭咩車（用嚟自動學識你嘅通勤習慣，可以喺設定關閉或者清除）
  • 路線資料快取同你最後所在位置（方便下次開 app 快啲顯示）

3. 定位

你俾 app 用定位之後，app 會用你嘅位置：

  • 搵附近車站同顯示地圖
  • 計步行同出門時間
  • 出門倒數期間追蹤你行到邊、幾時要落車

呢啲計算全部喺部機做，你嘅即時位置唔會傳去我哋或者任何伺服器。不過顯示地圖時，部機會向地政總署地圖伺服器下載你附近嗰一帶嘅地圖圖塊，所以地圖伺服器會知道你睇緊邊一區（同任何網上地圖一樣）。

你可以隨時喺手機設定收返定位權限；唔俾定位都可以手動搜尋地點同車站。

4. 登入帳戶（Google）

登入係自選嘅，用嚟喺換手機或者用多部機時記住你嘅設定。登入服務同雲端儲存由 Google 嘅 Firebase（Firebase Authentication 同 Cloud Firestore）提供。

由 Google 帳戶攞到：你嘅名、電郵地址、頭像網址同一個帳戶識別碼。我哋只用嚟顯示你登入咗邊個帳戶同分開每個用家嘅資料。

會同步上雲端：第 2 點列出嘅設定、常用地點（包括屋企／公司嘅位置、樓層）、常用路線、通勤紀錄、最近搜尋，同每樣資料最後修改時間。唔會同步你嘅即時或者最後所在位置、進行中嘅行程同快取。

邊個睇到：雲端資料設定咗安全規則，只有你自己登入咗先讀寫到。我哋唔會主動查閱，除非你要求我哋協助處理你嘅資料。

存喺邊：Google Cloud 喺香港嘅伺服器（asia-east2 區域）。

如果日後加入「用 Apple 登入」，處理方式相同；如果你揀咗「隱藏我的電郵」，我哋只會收到 Apple 提供嘅轉寄電郵。

5. App 用到嘅外部服務

為咗顯示實時資料，部機會直接連去以下服務。佢哋會見到你部機嘅 IP 地址同下面列出嘅內容，但唔會收到你嘅帳戶資料：

服務用途會收到乜
運輸署 DATA.GOV.HK、九巴、城巴、嶼巴、專線小巴、港鐵開放數據實時到站、交通消息車站同路線編號
香港天文台開放數據天氣警告冇額外資料
地政總署地圖 API、地址查詢服務（ALS）地圖、地址搜尋地圖範圍、你打嘅搜尋字
Photon（komoot，德國）地方名搜尋你打嘅搜尋字
hkbus.app下載路線資料冇額外資料
Google Fonts、cdnjs字型同地圖程式庫（網頁版）冇額外資料
Google Firebase登入同同步（只喺你登入過先會載入）見第 4 點

呢啲服務各自有自己嘅私隱政策。

6. 分享行程

你撳「分享行程」時，app 會砌一條連結，入面有路線、落車站、目的地名稱（例如「屋企」）同預計到達時間，再交俾你揀嘅 app（例如 WhatsApp）傳送。連結唔經我哋伺服器，亦都唔包括你嘅實時位置；但任何拎到條連結嘅人都會睇到以上內容。

7. 通知同行事曆

出門同落車通知喺部機產生。「加入行事曆提醒」會喺部機建立行事曆檔案，由你自己決定加唔加入行事曆。兩樣都唔經我哋伺服器。

8. 我哋唔會做嘅嘢

  • 唔會賣、租或者分享你嘅個人資料俾任何人作推廣用途
  • 冇廣告，冇第三方分析或者追蹤工具
  • 唔會用你嘅資料做同 app 功能無關嘅嘢

9. 保留同刪除

  • 部機資料：喺設定「清除紀錄」可以刪除通勤紀錄；刪除 app、或者清除瀏覽器網站資料，就會刪除所有部機資料。
  • 登出：雲端資料保留，部機資料亦保留。
  • 刪除帳戶：喺設定撳「刪除帳戶」，雲端上你嘅資料同 Firebase 帳戶會即刻刪除（部機嗰份保留，你可以另外清除）。
  • 帳戶會一直保留到你刪除為止。如果你冇辦法登入但想刪除帳戶，可以聯絡我哋。

10. 你嘅權利

根據香港《個人資料（私隱）條例》，你有權要求查閱同改正我哋持有嘅你嘅個人資料。大部分資料你都可以直接喺 app 入面睇到、修改或者刪除；其他要求請用下面方法聯絡我哋。

11. 兒童

App 唔係專為 13 歲以下兒童設計，我哋亦唔會刻意收集兒童嘅個人資料。

12. 政策更新

如果呢份政策有改動，我哋會更新呢頁同上面嘅生效日期；有重大改動會喺 app 入面通知。

13. 聯絡我哋

Privacy Policy

Effective 25 November 2026 · The Chinese version above prevails if the two differ.

  • No account needed. Without signing in, everything stays on your device.
  • Your live location is processed on your device only, to find nearby stops and time your trip. It is never uploaded.
  • If you sign in (Google), your settings, home/work places, pinned routes and commute history sync to your own cloud account, visible only to you.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics. We don't sell or share your data.
  • You can delete your account in the app at any time; cloud data is deleted immediately.

1. About this policy

Rushing HK (港住走, "the app") is a Hong Kong public transport arrivals and departure-countdown tool, available on the web and the App Store. This policy explains what the app collects, how it is used, where it is stored and how to delete it. The app is operated by [TO FILL: operator name] ("we").

2. Without an account: on your device only

Everything you set up is kept in your device's browser storage (or the app's own storage in the App Store version). We cannot see it. This includes your settings; saved places (home, school/work and custom places, with their names, coordinates, floor and lift); pinned and recently searched routes and destinations; commute history (when you tap "I've left" in the countdown: the time, origin, destination and route, used to learn your usual trips, and which you can turn off or clear in Settings); and caches, including your last known position.

3. Location

With your permission, the app uses your location to find nearby stops, show the map, estimate walking and leaving times, and follow your progress during a countdown. This happens on your device; your live location is not sent to us or any server. To draw the map, your device downloads map tiles for the surrounding area from the Lands Department map server, so that server can tell which area you are viewing, as with any online map. You can revoke location permission at any time and search for places manually instead.

4. Signing in (Google)

Signing in is optional. Sign-in and cloud storage are provided by Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore).

From your Google account we receive your name, email address, profile picture URL and an account ID, used only to show which account is signed in and to keep each user's data separate.

Synced to the cloud: the settings, saved places (including home/work coordinates and floor), pinned routes, commute history and recent searches listed in section 2, plus when each was last changed. Your live or last position, in-progress trips and caches are not synced.

Who can see it: security rules allow only you, while signed in, to read or write your data. We don't look at it unless you ask us to help with it.

Where it is stored: Google Cloud servers in Hong Kong (the asia-east2 region).

If Sign in with Apple is added later, it will be handled the same way; if you choose "Hide My Email", we only receive Apple's relay address.

5. Outside services the app talks to

To show live information your device connects directly to: DATA.GOV.HK and the KMB, Citybus, NLB, GMB and MTR open-data feeds (stop and route IDs); the Hong Kong Observatory (weather warnings); the Lands Department map API and Address Lookup Service (map area, your search text); Photon by komoot, Germany (your search text); hkbus.app (route data download); Google Fonts and cdnjs (fonts and map library, web version); and Google Firebase (only once you have signed in). These services see your IP address and the items listed, but not your account data. Each has its own privacy policy.

6. Sharing a trip

"Share trip" builds a link containing the route, alighting stop, destination name (for example "Home") and expected arrival time, and hands it to the app you choose, such as WhatsApp. The link does not pass through our servers and does not contain your live location, but anyone with the link can see that information.

7. Notifications and calendar

Leave and alight alerts are created on your device. "Add calendar alarms" creates a calendar file on your device that you choose whether to import. Neither goes through our servers.

8. What we don't do

We don't sell, rent or share your personal data for marketing. There are no ads and no third-party analytics or tracking. We don't use your data for anything unrelated to the app's features.

9. Keeping and deleting data

  • On your device: "Clear" in Settings deletes commute history; deleting the app or clearing the site's browser data removes everything.
  • Signing out keeps both your cloud and device data.
  • Delete account in Settings immediately deletes your cloud data and Firebase account (your device copy stays until you clear it).
  • Accounts are kept until you delete them. If you can't sign in but want your account deleted, contact us.

10. Your rights

Under Hong Kong's Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance you may ask to access and correct personal data we hold about you. Most of it you can view, edit or delete in the app yourself; for anything else, contact us below.

11. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we don't knowingly collect their personal data.

If this policy changes we will update this page and the effective date above, and announce significant changes in the app.
